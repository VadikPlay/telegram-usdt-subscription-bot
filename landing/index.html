The hard part

A TRC20 transfer has no memo field

If two subscribers each send 39.00 USDT to the same wallet, the blockchain cannot tell you who is who. Custodial services solve this by holding your money in their account. Gatekit solves it with arithmetic: every invoice gets a unique amount.

Advertised price
39.000000
What the subscriber sees on your pricing page.
Invoice GK-7F3A9 asks for
39.004271
The last digits identify the invoice. Under one cent of variance, 9999 unique slots.
Someone rounds it
39.000000 ✕
Not auto-credited. It is flagged to you in /unmatched with the sender and tx id, and you grant access manually. A guess is never made.
exact

Exact matching only

Amounts are compared with decimal equality. No floats anywhere in the payment path, no "close enough" tolerance that lets the wrong person in.

once

Replay-proof

A transaction id can only ever be credited once, so a re-read chain page cannot grant two subscriptions.

ton

TON matched by comment

TON transfers do carry a comment, so TON invoices match on the invoice code — a stronger guarantee than TRC20 allows.

late

Late and offline payments

Pays while the bot is down? On start it looks three days back. Pays after the invoice expires? A configurable tolerance still honours it.

The question everyone actually asks

Will it kick a paying member?

One wrong removal costs more than a year of saved fees: the chat sees it, people panic, subscriptions lapse. So removal is not an if expired: kick branch. Five independent guards must all agree before anyone loses access.

GuardWhat it does
SAFE_MODEOn by default. Nobody is ever removed — you only get told what would have happened. Run it this way for a week before enforcing.
Grace periodAccess continues for a configurable number of days after expiry, with a warning to the subscriber.
Payment protectionAnyone who paid within the protection window is never removed, whatever the dates say. Real money beats a stale expiry field.
IdempotencyA member already removed is never touched again. Restarts and overlapping sweeps cannot double-act.
Manual confirmOptional: every removal waits for an admin's click.
audit

Every decision on the record

Granted, reminded, entered grace, removed, removal refused — all appended to an audit log with the reason and whether a human or the scheduler did it. When a subscriber says they were treated unfairly, /audit settles it.

kick

Removal lets them return

Telegram's only kick primitive is a ban, so Gatekit bans and immediately unbans. A lapsed subscriber can resubscribe instead of being locked out forever.

test

Provable before you trust it

A self-test walks the entire money path against a fake chain and a fake Telegram — no token, no channel, no money. 53 unit tests and 56 end-to-end checks.

Deploy

One command, one file to fill in

# on any VPS with Docker
git clone https://github.com/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME/telegram-usdt-subscription-bot.git
cd telegram-usdt-subscription-bot
cp .env.example .env
nano .env        # BOT_TOKEN, CHANNEL_ID, ADMIN_IDS, TRON_WALLET
docker compose up -d

# rehearse the whole flow first — no token, no money needed
python scripts/selftest.py
# → invoice → exact payment → access → reminder → expiry → removal

SQLite, so there is no database to install. No inbound ports and no webhooks — Gatekit polls outbound only. A systemd unit ships alongside the Docker setup for people who prefer it.

Honest comparison

Gatekit is not always the right answer

Three real options. If one of the other two fits you better, use it — a tool that oversells itself is not worth trusting with your channel.

Telegram Star Subscriptions

Free · built in since Aug 2024

Native paid invite links, automatic renewal, automatic removal on non-payment. No server, no maintenance. The cost is the cut: buying Stars in the mobile app means roughly 30% to Apple or Google before Telegram's own withdrawal rate, leaving the owner around 65–68%. Via Fragment on desktop it is closer to 93–96%. Payouts reach a TON wallet through Fragment with a 21-day hold.

Best if your audience is not crypto-native.

A hosted service

~$19/mo flat · or 1–10% per sale

InviteMember, Subscriby, MyMembers, Whop and others are mature, supported products that work out of the box, and several accept crypto. Someone else is on call when something breaks at 3am.

Best if you want support, not control.

Gatekit

Free · MIT · you host it

Worth it when your subscribers already hold USDT, you want the money in your own wallet with no percentage and no hold period, you want the subscriber data in a database you control, or you need logic a hosted product will not build for you.

Requires a server, and you are the support team.

On Telegram's rules. Telegram's Bot Payments docs require Telegram Stars for digital goods sold through the Bot Payments API. Gatekit does not use that API: payment happens entirely on-chain, outside Telegram, and the bot only manages channel access. There is no explicit prohibition in the Terms of Service or Bot Developer Terms on accepting crypto for channel access, and services doing exactly this have run for years without being blocked.

It is still a grey area: Telegram reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to terminate a bot, its account and affiliated channels. Decide with that in your eyes open — if your channel is your livelihood and you want zero platform risk, use native Star Subscriptions.
